Privacy Policy

Art of Learning: Chinese · Last updated October 25, 2026

This policy explains what Art of Learning: Chinese (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and the choices you have. The app is operated by James Gruta. Contact: jgruta.hitachi@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Most of what you do (your settings, saved words and lessons, reading library, game scores and streaks) is stored on your device. You can use much of the app without an account.

What we collect, and why

  • Account information. If you sign in, we receive an account identifier and, depending on the sign-in method you choose, your email address and name. It is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google) and is used to recognise you and sync your data.
  • Synced progress. If you are signed in, your study progress, settings, and custom content are stored in Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so they follow you across devices.
  • Profile and friends (optional). If you create a profile, your display name, short bio and cartoon avatar are saved with your account. They are shared with other people only if you turn on sharing, and then only with people who have your friend code. You can turn sharing off at any time, which removes your shared profile and code.
  • Usage and diagnostics. We use Firebase services to understand which features are used and to fix problems (for example anonymous event counts and crash or performance information), and Firebase Remote Config to switch features on or off.
  • Advertising. The app may show optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the device’s advertising ID, IP address and similar technical data to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Ads only play if you choose to watch one. You can reset or disable your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Purchases. Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We receive confirmation of what you bought; we never see your payment card or bank details.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, the app sends study reminders and may receive push messages through Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Microphone. Used only if you choose speaking practice. Speech recognition is performed by your device’s speech service (which may be provided by Google); we do not keep recordings.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for anything other than running and improving the app.

Who processes data for us

Google (Firebase, AdMob and Google Play) processes data on our behalf under its own terms and privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

How long we keep it

Synced data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account or your data (see Delete your data), we delete it from our Firebase project. Backups and logs held by Google may take a short time to clear.

Your choices

  • Use the app without signing in.
  • Turn off profile sharing, or remove friends.
  • Delete your account and data at any time.
  • Turn off notifications or reset your advertising ID in system settings.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may also have the right to ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data. Write to jgruta.hitachi@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google’s Firebase. No method is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app.
