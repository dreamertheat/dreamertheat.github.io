Delete your account and data

Art of Learning: Chinese · Last updated October 25, 2026

You can delete your Art of Learning: Chinese account and the data we hold about it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and go to Settings.
  2. In the sync / account section, tap Delete my cloud data.
  3. Confirm.

This removes your synced progress, your profile, your shared profile and friend code, and your friend list from our servers. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Delete the sign-in account too, or if you can't open the app

Email jgruta.hitachi@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your account and everything linked to it. We will do so within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted, what is kept

  • Deleted: account identifier, synced progress and settings, profile (name, bio, avatar), friend code and friend list.
  • Not ours to delete: purchase records kept by Google Play, and ad or diagnostic data held by Google under its own policies.
